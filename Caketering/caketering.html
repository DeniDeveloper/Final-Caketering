Our Journey

Caketering started in 2026 from a small home kitchen in Zamboanga City. What began as weekend baking experiments quickly grew into a beloved local bakery known for creative designs and delicious flavors.

Today, we're proud to serve hundreds of happy customers across Zamboanga City. Every cake that leaves our kitchen is made with the same love and attention to detail as that very first one.

We believe that every celebration deserves a cake that's as unique as the moment itself. Whether it's a simple birthday or an elaborate wedding, we're here to make it special.
