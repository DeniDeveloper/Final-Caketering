Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about ordering, lead times, payments, and care.

How far in advance should I order custom cakes?

For standard catalog cakes, we accept same-day and next-day orders. For bespoke custom designs and multi-tier celebration cakes, we recommend ordering 3 to 5 days in advance so our bakers and decorators have sufficient time to sculpt and cure your personalized details.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept GCash, Maya, Credit/Debit Cards, Bank Transfer (BDO/BPI), and Cash on Delivery (COD) for orders below ₱2,000 within Zamboanga City.

How are the cakes delivered safely in Zamboanga City?

Every cake is packaged inside reinforced heavy-duty cake boxes placed in insulated temperature-controlled cold carriers. Our dedicated bakery dispatch riders are trained to handle tiered structures carefully.

How should I store my cake after receiving it?

Keep fresh cream and chiffon cakes refrigerated until 30 minutes before serving. Fondant cakes should be kept in a cool, air-conditioned room away from direct heat or humidity.
